**4.2. What are the pitfalls of using List in SwiftUI, and how do you mitigate them?**

    • id stability issues
    • performance issues with complex views
  • Use List → when you need native iOS look, swipe actions, or editing
  • Use LazyVStack → when you need full customization with performance
  • Use VStack → only for small, fixed number of items
