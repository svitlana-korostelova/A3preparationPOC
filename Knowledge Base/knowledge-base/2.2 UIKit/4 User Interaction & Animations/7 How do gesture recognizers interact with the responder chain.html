How do gesture recognizers interact with the responder chain?

Gesture recognizers sit alongside the responder chain, not inside it - they attach to a view and receive raw touches from UIKit's touch dispatch in parallel with (and typically before) the view's own touchesBegan/Moved/Ended.

  • UIKit hit-tests once per touch to find the hit-test view, then delivers the touch both to that view and to every gesture recognizer attached to it or any of its superviews.
  • A recognizer that reaches "recognized" can cancel touch delivery to the view (and thus the responder chain) via cancelsTouchesInView (default true) - why a cell's touchesBegan can "disappear" once a pan/swipe recognizes.
  • delaysTouchesBegan/delaysTouchesEnded control whether touches are held back from the view while the recognizer decides.
  • Multiple recognizers negotiate via UIGestureRecognizerDelegate: shouldRecognizeSimultaneouslyWith, shouldRequireFailureOf, shouldBeRequiredToFailBy.

So the responder chain (UIResponder → next responder → ... → UIApplication) decides who handles an event after hit-testing; gesture recognizers are a separate, competing consumer of the same raw touch stream, and typically win by cancelling the responder chain's delivery.
